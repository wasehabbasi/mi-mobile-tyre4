Mobile Puncture & Tyre Repair

Safe, professional repairs carried out at your convenience

Can Your Tyre Be Repaired?

Not all damaged tyres need full replacement. We strictly adhere to British Standard BS AU 159 rules to ensure your safety:

Repairable Conditions

  • Puncture is in the central 75% of tread area
  • Puncture diameter is smaller than 6mm
  • No previous improper repair attempts
  • Sidewall and internal structure are undamaged

Requires Replacement

  • Damage located on the tyre sidewall/shoulder
  • Tread depth is below legal limit (1.6mm)
  • Secondary rubber structural damage from driving flat
